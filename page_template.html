fMRI-to-Video Reconstructions

Each card shows the clip the subject watched (left) next to the reconstruction chosen by the brain-only reranker from 100 candidates (right). Both play in sync at the same duration and resolution. Examples are grouped by quality according to the official metrics.

Metrics per example: 50-way identification accuracy for video and frames, and SSIM. Reconstructions are resized to the ground-truth resolution without cropping.
